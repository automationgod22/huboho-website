The space

Pick your corner

The verandah

A bamboo-mat ceiling with a wavy white trim, cane chairs, woven sun plates on white clapboard, and more plants than we can name. It's the spot for slow Sunday lunches, dogs stretched out under the table, and photos in the good light.

Good for Sunday lunch · golden hour · you and your dog

Indoors

Rattan lanterns, cognac leather chairs, macramé, wobbly-edged mirrors and the AC on for those May afternoons. One wall reminds you that you're always a wildflower child. The sun poster adds: protect your inner peace.

Good for date night · birthdays · a long catch-up
